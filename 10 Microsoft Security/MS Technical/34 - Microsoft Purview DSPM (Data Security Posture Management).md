# Microsoft Purview DSPM (Data Security Posture Management)

## Summary
DSPM gives a central view of data-security posture — what sensitive data you have, where it is, who can access it and where it's exposed — the check most organisations skip before deploying Copilot.

## Key facts
- Unifies Purview signals into a posture view across traditional apps and AI apps/agents.
- DSPM for AI is the 'front door' for securing AI interactions, with recommendations and one-click policies.
- A sensitivity-labelling schema is NOT required for DSPM, but significantly improves context, reporting and recommendations.
- Surfaces oversharing risk before Copilot can amplify it.

## Licensing & prerequisites
- Microsoft 365 E5 / E5 Compliance; DSPM currently in preview for parts of the experience.

## Practitioner notes / gotchas
- Deploying Copilot without a data-security check first is a common, avoidable mistake.
- Correct the myth that 'labels are the foundation of all of Purview' — DSPM works without them (labels just help).
