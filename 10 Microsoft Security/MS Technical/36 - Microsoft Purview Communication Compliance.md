# Microsoft Purview Communication Compliance

## Summary
Communication Compliance detects regulatory and business-conduct violations across communication channels — email, Teams, Copilot and custom generative-AI apps — the conversations DLP cannot see.

## Key facts
- Detects sensitive-info sharing, harassment, threats, and inappropriate/adult content.
- Covers email, Teams, Microsoft 365 Copilot and custom generative-AI apps.
- Privacy by default: pseudonymised usernames, role-based access.
- Policy templates and remediation workflows for flagged communications.

## Licensing & prerequisites
- Microsoft 365 E5 / E5 Compliance.

## Practitioner notes / gotchas
- DLP protects data; Communication Compliance governs conduct in the communication itself.
- Now extends into AI/Copilot conversations, not just email/Teams.
