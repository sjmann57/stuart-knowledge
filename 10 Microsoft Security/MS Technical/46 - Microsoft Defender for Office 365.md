# Microsoft Defender for Office 365

## Summary
Defender for Office 365 protects email and collaboration against phishing, business email compromise and malicious links/attachments — the email security gap often hiding unused inside an M365 licence.

## Key facts
- Safe Links and Safe Attachments detonate URLs/files; anti-phishing protects against impersonation and spoofing.
- Plan 1 = protection (Safe Links/Attachments, anti-phish); Plan 2 adds Threat Explorer, Attack Simulation Training, automated investigation & response (AIR).
- Alerts and AIR feed Defender XDR incidents.
- User-reported phishing feeds the Security Copilot Phishing/Alert Triage Agent.

## Licensing & prerequisites
- Plan 1 vs Plan 2; Plan 2 included in Microsoft 365 E5. Preset security policies (Standard/Strict) speed configuration.

## Practitioner notes / gotchas
- The classic scenario: finance receives a 'CEO' email — impersonation protection is what catches it.
- Owning E5 isn't enough; the policies (ideally preset Standard/Strict) still need turning on.
