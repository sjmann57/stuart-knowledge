# Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management

## Summary
Insider Risk Management detects risky user behaviour that DLP's content rules can't see — data theft before departure, mass download/exfiltration, policy violations — using behavioural signals with privacy controls.

## Key facts
- Policy templates (e.g. data theft by departing users, data leaks, risky AI usage).
- Behavioural signals from M365 plus HR/third-party connectors; pseudonymised by default with role-based access.
- Risk scoring and case management for investigation.
- Risky AI usage template detects prompt-injection attempts and access to protected material.

## Licensing & prerequisites
- Microsoft 365 E5 / E5 Compliance (Insider Risk Management).

## Practitioner notes / gotchas
- DLP sees content and actions; IRM sees behaviour and intent over time — they answer different questions.
- Privacy-by-design (pseudonymisation, RBAC) matters for stakeholder buy-in.
