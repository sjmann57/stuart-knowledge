# Microsoft Defender XDR — five products, not yet a platform

## Summary
Early framing of Defender XDR: at the time it looked like five separate Defender products sharing a portal rather than one coordinated platform. The direction of travel (later realised) was toward genuine cross-domain correlation and unified incidents.

## Key facts
- Defender XDR spans endpoints (MDE), email (MDO), identity (MDI) and cloud apps (MDCA), correlated in the Microsoft Defender portal.
- The value is cross-domain correlation into a single incident, not each product in isolation.
- Later evolution: unified SecOps with Sentinel, Exposure Management and attack disruption (see Day 91).

## Licensing & prerequisites
- Correlation only covers the products you have licensed and provisioned.

## Practitioner notes / gotchas
- Owning the components isn't the same as them working as a platform — integration and configuration is the job.
- Superseded by the Day 91 note, which reflects the matured unified-incidents + attack-disruption model.
