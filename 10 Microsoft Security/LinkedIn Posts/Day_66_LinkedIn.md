## LinkedIn Article (published — 2026-08-09)

Not everything is always perfect

As I sit drinking my coffee and reflecting on the week past, my posts, work, family and interactions with friends, none of these are perfect.

I do not have a life without stress, conflict, disappointment, fear and self-doubt.

Many of us have these feelings, and in a world where there is pressure to have the perfect picture or post, say the right thing, wear the right clothes and then be exposed to potential criticism, often from people who have never met you, it can have an impact.

So, as I sit here, how do I see this? How do I find ways to help myself and, in doing that, potentially help others?

For me, as with many people, I have an outer shell, a suit of armour. But negative things can still play on my mind.

How did that person see me?
Did I say that right?
Did I come across the way I wanted?

I sometimes find all of this difficult, and I know others do too.

So what is the answer, if there is one?

First, be an example. If someone's opinion is different from yours, that's okay. Listen, ask questions and find out more. You may still not agree, but that's okay. They may not agree with you either.

Look for the good in others.

In my roles in Scouting, I often have to look past where people are now and more towards what they are capable of. I try to look at what I believe a person can achieve, just as others have done when looking at me.

Build your armour.

That may be a life partner who stands by you, a friend who gives you support, a mentor who guides you or a life coach who helps you gain perspective.

I thankfully have many of those people in my life, and I am sure I could name a few others who have helped me along the way.

Another part is who I associate with. Look at the people around you. They will have a strong influence on how you adapt, grow, think and react.

The other tools I use are the books I read, mindset tools and habits that have worked for me. Some work better than others, and I am still learning.

My armour isn't about pretending that things don't affect me.
They do.
It's about having people, habits and experiences around me that help me deal with them, learn from them and keep moving forward.

So, what forms part of your armour?

What helps you deal with self-doubt, criticism, disappointment or those days when things simply don't go to plan?

What tips can you share?

You will almost certainly have something I haven't thought of, haven't tried, or perhaps tried unsuccessfully. Your approach might just help me, or someone else reading this.
