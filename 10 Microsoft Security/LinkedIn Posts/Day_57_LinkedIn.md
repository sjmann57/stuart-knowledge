## LinkedIn Post (published — 2026-07-31)

Happy Friday! What makes you smile?

I often spend a lot of time on video calls, and everyone loses concentration. So if my background is visible, then that often becomes a talking point or an icebreaker.

For the past 12-ish years, I have worked from my conservatory all year round. In the winter it can be like an ice box 🥶, and the summer just below the boiling point🔥🥵

But what I always get comments on is where I work, bikes, medals, and other random things in my background and on my desk.

It adds a talking point, breaks the ice, and often raises a few laughs.

So what random things do I have? An Inspector Gadget figurine, ducks, lots of ducks, coconut, puzzles, teddy bear, magnetic ball bearing, yo-yo and much more

But the one thing that always gets asked about is why you have a duck in a tutu?

The duck, tutu, hat, and glasses have all come from my adventures in Scouting.

The hat goes with me on most camps and activities; I have a reputation around my hats 😆

The other items are from large international Jamborees I have taken part in, and each has a story behind it.

Today's thoughts.

Life is for living, and always embracing the inner child is my advice whenever you can.

The inner child keeps you young, with a perspective on the world that looks for positivity and makes you someone people want to be around. A smile costs little but goes such a long way.

All this was triggered on a call with my previous boss, who is now working in a partner organisation. As I now have a professional always on the background, he asked, "Where is the duck?"

So here is the duck! 🤣
