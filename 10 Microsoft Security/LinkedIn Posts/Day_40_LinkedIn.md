## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's published version — 2026-07-13)

So, where am I going…?

After speaking with some brilliant companies (and the rare luxury of choosing between multiple offers), I'm delighted to share that I'm joining [CyberOne](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cyberone/) as a Senior Microsoft 365 Security Consultant, reporting to [Luke Elston](https://www.linkedin.com/in/luelston/)

What drew me to CyberOne? They're a Microsoft Security Solutions Partner and MISA member with Microsoft Security genuinely at the core of everything they do, from Entra, Defender XDR and Sentinel through to Purview and data security. It was also great to meet future colleagues on Thursday, before starting on Monday; it totally validated my decission; the culture came across as friendly, supportive, and driven to always improve. That's exactly where I want to be.

What I'm looking forward to:

🔐 Helping more organisations mature their Microsoft Security posture
📊 Guiding customers on their data security and compliance journey
🤝 Joining a team of passionate experts who live and breathe their chosen expertise


CyberOne's core values say a lot about why this move felt right:

✅ Customer Defence Focussed
✅ Relentless Innovation
✅ Act Kind, Be Kind
✅ Be Awesome, Demand Awesome
✅ Execute 100%

Thank you to everyone who spoke with me during the process; the calibre of companies and the people that made it a hard decision, which is a good problem to have.

Here's to the next chapter. 🚀
