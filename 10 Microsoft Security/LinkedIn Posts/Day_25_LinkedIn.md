## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's version — published)

Sunday thoughts, who is it that always has your corner? I was thinking about some of the challenges I have gone through, with some more recent ones, and how I get through them. Rarely do you face challenges alone, without support. Like many things, be it work or personal, life is often a journey from one challenge to another, working through them as best we can. A mentor of mine once told me, "Life is not fair and meant to be a challenge; if it's not a challenge, you're in a box". I have faced many challenges, some of which have been very difficult in both my professional and personal life. I am very lucky to have Jeanie in my corner. Often, just to listen and ask questions to help focus my thinking, and at times, that may be all you need. If you, like me, have someone who is always in your corner, make sure you thank them, as you will need them again at some point, hopefully not too soon. Thank you Jeanie!
