## LinkedIn Post (scheduled — 2026-08-23)

I am often so busy, always say yes, I will sort that, but.

The "but" sometimes comes when I reflect and realise I should have said no.

If I had said no and didn't volunteer for something at work, home or Scouting, what would I have done instead?

Today, I was thinking about why I say yes so often, and maybe I should have just said no.

It is how I am wired

I seem to want to help others; it is a strength and a weakness.

The same could be said if it were the other way round.

I keep saying I will say no more often, but I don't, and I sometimes end up burning the candle at both ends because I do what I say, well, most of the time; I am human.

So for today, I am going to keep being me, not insist on saying no because I reflected on it, and being different.

I am just going to be happy I am who I am; it's the honest answer, not one because this is social media and I'm suddenly going to change.

So today, I'm going to escape my laptop and phone, chill, enjoy the day, and have a wonderful Sunday.
