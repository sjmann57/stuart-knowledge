## LinkedIn Post (published — 2026-07-28)

Microsoft SC-500, so how did the beta exam go for me?

Well, I can say my preparation may not have been the best; my planned revision didn't really happen, and the test day came around with no option to move it, so it was down to what I knew and my experience.

I have already shared my experience of the exam and my thoughts, which I hope gave others insight and inspiration.

At school, exams definitely were not my thing; I thought when I left, that would be it, no more exams. How naive of me; I have taken more exams than I could ever imagine, some I thought might be beyond me, like ISC2 CSSP. Like many who find things challenging, I turn to them; they make me grow as a person.

With all that said, I am maybe surprised, a little shocked maybe, but extremely happy; I have passed the SC-500, and that adds to my tally of passed exams.

So if you are going to sit any exam, my advice is to stay calm, breathe, and read the question twice. After making my choice of answer, I do not go back and change it; I have faith that when I scroll to the next question, that was my best answer.

Any advice you would give when taking exams?

Good Luck in your next one!
