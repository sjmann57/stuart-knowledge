## LinkedIn Post (published — 2026-09-04)

Success is not a destination

When I was younger, success was passing exams, getting a job, getting a pay rise or solving a problem.

In every way, they were all successes.

But how do I see success now?

Now, I see those successes as part of my story and how I move through life.

I often talk about successes on a Friday, and I think we need to celebrate them all. Sometimes it might be with a coffee, other times a meal out.

But they are not the destination or the final stop.

They are stepping stones.

What I want to ask you today is this:

Where are your stepping stones taking you, and do you even know?

Many people I speak to know exactly where they are going. Athletes and business owners are good examples. They often have a clear goal and a path towards it.

Celebrate your successes, but also know the path you are walking.

This week, my daily posts have given me some small successes. They haven't only added towards my posting goal, they have also taught me some great lessons.

I had the pleasure of talking about some of those wins with friends this week.

One thing I realised was that it took me more than 50 days of posting every day before I started to see real traction in the community I was trying to work with.

Imagine if I had stopped at day 30 because I thought it wasn't working.

That, for me, is the point of goals, wins and daily successes.

Individually, they might seem small.

But keep putting those stepping stones together, and eventually you can look back and see just how far you have travelled.

So this Success Friday, celebrate your wins.

But perhaps also ask yourself:

Where are they taking you?
