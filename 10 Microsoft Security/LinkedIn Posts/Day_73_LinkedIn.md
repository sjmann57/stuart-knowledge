## LinkedIn Post (published — 2026-08-16)

We all need to take a break

Scouting has been a lifelong obsession and is part of who I am.

Over the summer, we typically take a break and enjoy some non-Scouting time.

Taking this time away is still quite new for me. Since joining my current Group, it is only the third Scout Group I have belonged to since starting as an eight-year-old Cub.

I now look forward to the summer break. For four weeks, I completely let go of the jobs and responsibilities, giving me time to recharge.

Sometimes, it is important to step away and disconnect.

I must admit, I usually return excited, with a wave of new ideas filling my imagination about how we can make our adventures even better.

Whatever your passion, taking some time away from it may be exactly what you need.

As business owners, employees, Scouts, and sportspeople, we all need to balance several parts of our lives. Taking a break, even briefly, helps me recharge. I know it works for me.

I would like to hear your ideas, as they may help me and others.

What advice would you give someone who wants to recharge and return re-energised?
