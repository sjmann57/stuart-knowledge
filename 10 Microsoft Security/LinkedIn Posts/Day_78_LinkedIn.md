## LinkedIn Post (published — 2026-08-21)

Take a moment to pause

On Fridays, I often think about the week and my wins, but today it's a little different.

So, this Friday morning, as I write this, I have just finished a mobility and strength session on Zoom with my friends, and my house is quiet.

This time in the morning, between exercise and the start of my working day, is a magical time for me.

The quiet gives me a few minutes to reflect on yesterday, think about today and remind myself of the things I am grateful for.

Sometimes I write them down. Sometimes I just hold them in my mind.

Not every day goes as you want or plan, but take time to be grateful.

Do it every day if you can. It's good for the soul.

They don't need to be big, dramatic things. Just something you are grateful for.

And don't judge your list for today. Tomorrow it will be different again.

The more you practise this, the easier it gets.

There are lots of books and podcasts about the subject, and they cover it much better than I could.

Today's 10 things:

1. Jeanie cooking dinner.
2. My two oldest friends checking in on our WhatsApp group.
3. Going for a walk after dinner last night with Jeanie.
4. A photo from a holiday 20 years ago of my kids, those special moments in life.
5. Feeling really happy about a client call and the feedback yesterday.
6. My knees not aching as I walked upstairs to bed last night.
7. Sleeping well.
8. How confident my son is.
9. How determined my daughter is.
10. I turned up again for my challenge.

Often, I am grateful simply to sit in the garden and listen to the sounds around me.

Take a few minutes now.

Think of, or write down, five things you are grateful for.

If you want to share one, I would really like to hear yours.
