## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's version — published)

Saturday, a day to get things done, put some things to one side, and pick up others. Mindset and working on yourself shouldn't take a day off.
My day started off with getting out on my bike and cycling with friends. Very good for the body and soul.
Mindset needs to be consistent and ever-present. For me, that starts with what I feed my mind. Books have been part of my life for a long time, and audiobooks have changed how I fit them into the day. A commute, time in the car, walk at lunchtime. There is rarely a reason not to learn something and keep your mind and internal voice in check.
The books that have shaped how I think most are not always the obvious ones. Some of the most valuable lessons have come from titles I almost did not pick up. The right book at the right moment can shift how you see a problem, a situation, or yourself.
Mentors are an important part of my own journey. My mentors have come in many different guises, from my first boss, Norman Shakes, to friends such as Jose Lazo, my life coach, Sarah Paskin, and many more. You need to find people who can see a bigger picture and stretch you to grow into the person they see you being. Never underestimate the power of having a mentor to support you in the various areas of your life.
The combination of the right books and the right people in your corner is powerful. Neither works as well without the other. And both require you to show up and do the work, not just once, but consistently.
I looked through the books in my Audible library, and I have 94 titles, which I was pleasantly surprised to see. With my subscription, I add a new book each month, a good way to keep learning and growing.
These are some of my mindset books:
Will It Make the Boat Go Faster, Surrounded by Idiots, I May Be Wrong, The Diary of a CEO, Atomic Habits, Manifest, Feel the Fear and Do It Anyway, Who Not How, The Values Factor, Limitless, The Miracle Morning, Secrets of the Millionaire Mind, The High 5 Habit, Rich Dad Poor Dad, Fear Less, Make Time, Change Your Paradigm Change Your Life, The Mountain Is You, Burn the Bullsh*t, Profit First, The Compound Effect, The Luck Factor, Go for No!, The Kindness Method, Never Finished, Freedom from Anxiety, Zero Negativity, Can't Hurt Me, Unleash the Power Within, The Art of Resilience, The Fear Bubble, Psycho-Cybernetics, Tame Your Inner Critic, Who Moved My Cheese, The Magic of Thinking Big, The Richest Man in Babylon...In Action, Think and Grow Rich, The Best of Les Brown, How to Win Friends & Influence People, You Are a Badass, The Decision, Becoming Bulletproof, Mind Over Muscle, Zero Negativity, High Performance, Sitting Pretty, Stolen Focus.
I also have a long list of autobiographies, hearing about how people went through struggles and still achieved. I do like a good autobiography.
