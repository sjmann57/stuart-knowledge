## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's version — published 2026-07-19)

Finally, I have a weekend that is not an extension of my week, aka back to a Monday-to-Friday thing, so what is today about?

Today feels like a normal day: off out with friends on my bike and talking about all things; highs, lows and our stories of the past 7 days, but 11 years ago….

11 years ago, my day was a bit more of a challenge: it was my first 2.4-mile swim, first 112 miles, and first marathon, and I did it all on the same day.

That marked the completion of 12 months of training, and not without challenges, such as falling off my bike weeks before race day and being unable to walk very far due to injuries, let alone cycle or run.
Determination, good physio and a coach made the difference.

Early morning runs, late-night swims, weekend fitting-in sessions around family time. You need to do what you need to do when your goal is to complete your first Ironman.

Having a goal with a firm date and a set of mini goals along the way makes a difference.
The two things that really got me there were being accountable myself and having my coach, Sarah Paskin (who still life coaches me today).
The other is the team you surround yourself with, and for me, they needed to be like-minded, supportive, and accountable in both directions.

What does this have to do with professional social media, cybersecurity and LinkedIn?

Knowing what you want, why you want it, giving your goal a date, and having the right people around you is no different in any work or professional situation.
The most successful people have laser focus, coaches, mentors, and a dynamic plan to reach their goals.

Actions for today:
Know what you want and why
What is the date? Set the goal
Who will support you?
What is the plan to achieve it?
Vocalise that goal
