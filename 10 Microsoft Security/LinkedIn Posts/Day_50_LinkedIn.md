## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's version — published 2026-07-25)

It's not success but a milestone, Friday!

So many of you have been seeing my posts, and for some, it feels like you see them every time you open LinkedIn. Something I heard again today.

Today, my milestone is 50 consecutive posts or articles over 50 days. As you may have seen a while ago, I am sharing what I know about Microsoft Security and telling some of my story and thoughts.

The process has
* Got me a new role at CyberOne that I am really enjoying.
* Connected with many new people
* Had some great conversations
* Spent time improving how I write and present my message
* Learnt new things about my subject matter and myself
* Remembered some great memories and learnt a few things about myself.

There is more to come
* I have vocalised my goal of 100 posts in 100 days, so another 50 posts to come
* More to learn as I share each day
* A need to continue to grow and improve
* Thinking of new ways to share my content

What drives you?
Have you told others your goals?

Telling others and making them public gives you fewer places to hide, but also, for yourself, makes it real. It's easy to set a goal, tell no one and then reset the date.

Be accountable at least to yourself and preferably to a mentor, partner, family member, your boss, or to the world on social media.

But be kind to yourself; not every goal will you achieve in the time you set. I have a few of those!

In the past 50 days, I have had long days, meaning I ended up being busy writing, still going at 9 pm to hit my daily goal.

What are you going to do?
Tell me your goal; if not in the comments, feel free to message me the goal and the date to achieve it
