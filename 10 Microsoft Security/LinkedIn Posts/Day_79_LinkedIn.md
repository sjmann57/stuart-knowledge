## LinkedIn Post (published — 2026-08-22)

The middle. The part no one notices or talks about.

The start of anything is exciting.

It is like being a child. Everything is new, the days are fun, and they are filled with new adventures.

Then there is the end.

The achievement is complete. The finish line is crossed. Everyone is excited. The race has been run, the mission is complete, and you can relax and enjoy knowing your work is done.

But what about the middle?

The middle is where we spend much of our time.

We take it one step at a time.

No one is necessarily cheering. No one notices every bit of work being done or sees the struggles along the way.

I am in the middle part of my 100-day challenge.

Today is day 79.

The excitement of hitting day 10, 20 or 50 has gone.

I am not yet at day 93, with just one week to go, when you know the work is almost done.

I am simply here, in the middle, where another post turns up that you may or may not be interested in.

I have reflected on this part of my self-imposed challenge, and I often take it back to my Ironman races.

By this stage, I would have completed the fun part, for me, of the swim, getting out of the water around the one-hour mark.

I would have jumped on the bike, taken on the hills, enjoyed the scenery and fuelled for the final part.

This is the run.

42.2 km.

Normally on a looped course.

For many, this is the tough part.

Your mind tells you to stop running.

Walk for a bit.

Take an extra moment at the feed station.

Away from the start and finish line, there are fewer people cheering you on. In your mind, you can start wondering if anyone is supporting you at all.

This is the middle.

And that is where I am in my challenge.

When you get here, there is what is happening in your mind, and then there is what others actually see.

You may feel no one is watching, supporting you or calling out your name.

But people are still watching.

Now is the time to have faith in what you started and enjoy where you are.

Keep stepping forward.

Don't listen to the voice telling you to stop.

Have the same faith and passion you had when you passed that first milestone.

If you are undertaking any job, task or challenge, don't give up in the middle because you think no one is watching, taking notice or interested.

Keep going.

Get through this stage.

Finish what you started.

Then, as I have spoken about before, celebrate.

Celebrate every win and be grateful for your own achievement.

I often tell everyone what I am going to do.

Why?

Because if I tell everyone, I have nowhere to hide when I get to the middle.

I have said this will get done.

And it will.

What are you in the middle of?

What are you going to do to get to the finish?

It could be a job at home, a project you are working on, a physical challenge…

Or 100 posts in 100 days.
