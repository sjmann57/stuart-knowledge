## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's version — published)

The image is of me finishing Ironman Lanzarote in May 2024. The image is great; the day was one of the very best I've had completing my 3rd Ironman, but it's not about the finish line.

I was reflecting today on the habits needed to pull off events like this. It's getting out of bed daily to work out at 6 a.m. on Zoom with the 'Morning Movers'. It's the small daily habits that get us to the top of our own mountains, the one step after another.

What are your daily habits to help you climb your mountain?

I am fortunate to have the help and guidance of a coach who is not just about physical fitness, but about being fit for life. Remember to surround yourself with people who lift and support you, challenge your thinking and ask the questions you need to hear, not those who undermine and devalue you. I know I have a good network of support, mentors, and, importantly, friends. Make sure you are always building your own network to support you, not only on good days but also on challenging ones.

Happy Friday
