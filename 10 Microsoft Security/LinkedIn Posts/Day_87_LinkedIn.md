## LinkedIn Post (scheduled — 2026-08-30)

What does Sunday mean to you?

Sunday can mean something different to everyone, and perhaps something different to you depending on where you are in life.

It could be the first day of the week, a fresh start rather than the end of the weekend.

It could be a day of rest, permitting yourself to stop without feeling guilty.

Maybe it's a day of reflection, thinking about the week that's gone and what you've learnt.

Or perhaps it's a day of celebration, appreciating people, experiences and the small wins that often get overlooked.

Which Sunday do you need today?

* When I was younger, Sunday was a family day.
* The shops were closed, everyone had chores around the house, and then we'd all go out in the afternoon.
* I loved my Sundays.
* It was time spent with my family. A day when we all sat around the table, laughed and joked together.

Things are different now.

The shops are open, everyone is busy, and Sunday can almost become just another day.

Life is fast. Everyone seems short on time, and Sunday can easily become the day when you try to get all the jobs done that you haven't managed during the week.

My children have grown up now, but we still often go out on a Sunday evening to eat. Or I'll head out with Jeanie for a walk, a coffee and, quite possibly, a slice of cake.

Sundays for me are a time to take a moment.

Although, when my children were younger, Sundays certainly weren't always restful.

They were busy.

But they were busy together.

Looking back, that's a time in my life I really cherished, and I'm glad we made that time for each other.

There's been a bit of a theme to my posts over the last few days.

Friday was about finding your groove when you're struggling to get things done and don't feel as creative as you normally do.

Saturday was about Positive Mental Attitude, and how having a positive mindset doesn't mean you always feel positive. Sometimes you have to find ways through the challenges, and sometimes you have to accept that they're there.

So those two pieces of the jigsaw bring me to Sunday.

For me, it's a chance to recharge, find my mojo and ground myself ready for whatever comes next.

We spend a lot of time planning what we need to do.

Sunday is a good day to ask a different question.

What do I actually need today?

* Rest?
* Family?
* Reflection?
* Celebration?

Maybe just a walk, a coffee and a slice of cake.

What makes Sunday special for you?
