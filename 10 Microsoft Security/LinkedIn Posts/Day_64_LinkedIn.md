## LinkedIn Article (published — 2026-08-07)

Success Friday is back today, 7th August.

The last seven days have been full of small wins. They are easy to overlook unless you stop and reflect. Today is day 64 of writing on LinkedIn, and that is one of those wins. It is a reminder that progress often comes from showing up consistently, one day at a time.

Take five minutes today to reflect on what has gone well in your own life.
One habit I have built is spending a few minutes each morning reflecting on what is good in my life. It changes how I approach the day and helps me keep things in perspective.

This week I finished listening to Embrace the CHAOS by Jason Fox. The book closes with a 52-week challenge to help keep the learning alive rather than letting it fade away. One idea that stayed with me was learning to keep looking forward, even when life becomes chaotic. Thinking about it afterwards, I realised I probably do that more than I had given myself credit for.

Building daily habits to keep my attitude facing the right direction is a choice. It does not matter what life throws at me. Get back up, take one more step forward and keep moving.

I have also had some great conversations following this week's posts and those from previous weeks. A number of people have sent direct messages asking about careers, learning, confidence and how to approach different situations. It is humbling to be asked for advice and to be trusted enough to share how I think through decisions.

I rarely tell people what they should do. Instead, I explain how I approach a decision and ask questions that help them think through their own situation.

It is easy to draw on your own experiences, but everyone has different goals, different circumstances and different priorities. Sometimes the best thing you can do is help someone think more clearly rather than tell them what path to take.

I find the same approach works when talking with clients. Listening first and understanding their position helps shape the questions I ask next. Until you can see things from their point of view, it is difficult to offer advice, guidance or support that genuinely helps.

Another win this week has been watching Jeanie Mann take the next step in building her business, Rivoli Stays. After taking time to pause, consolidate and reflect, she is moving towards the goals she set herself for the year. Watching someone you care about make progress towards something they have worked hard for is always worth celebrating.

So, after all that, here are my top five wins this week:
* Helping others on their Microsoft journey.
* Supporting a client as they plan the next stage of their Microsoft Security programme.
* Watching Jeanie move her business forward.
* Reaching day 64 of my 100-day LinkedIn writing challenge.
* Enjoying lunchtime walks in the sunshine, taking time to reflect and feeling grateful for my family and friends.

What are your wins for the week?
