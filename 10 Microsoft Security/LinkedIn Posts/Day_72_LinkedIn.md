## LinkedIn Post (published — 2026-08-15)

As I write Day 72 at the top of my page, it brings back memories of the Big Brother House, the first year it ran, and how we were all gripped by the show and Marcus Bentley, the voice of the show.

"Day 72 in the Big Brother House…"

It made me smile and think back, as we often do, to what felt like an easier time.

Writing every day has its highs and lows.

There is that moment when I sit down, look at a blank page and think:

What will I write about today? Do I actually have any ideas?

Well, today I don't.

So that is my idea for today.

When you look at any endurance activity, there are times when you wonder about the choices you made and if there is any point in carrying on.

That is a natural and honest feeling.

But I have to come back to why I started and what impact I may have had along the way.

When things feel difficult, it's easy to walk away from my kitchen table, take my coffee, and do something else.

This week, I was reminded about procrastination. The thought process behind it and the impact it can have.

So, I'm taking that thought and sitting here until this is done.

Life doesn't always follow the path you expected, and motivation will not always be there.

Sometimes there is no one cheering next to you in the race.

It is just you and the task at hand.

What I have learned is that you cannot always wait for motivation.

Sometimes you have to take action first.

Take action. Keep moving. Have some fun along the way.

The motivation and drive often come back once you start.

So today, that job you have put off, the call you have avoided, or the item on your to-do list that keeps slipping further down the list:

Get it done.

Then celebrate the simple fact that you took action.
