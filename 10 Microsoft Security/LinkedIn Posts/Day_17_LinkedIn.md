## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's version — published)

Water Orton Carnival

In the village, I help run the Scout Group, and it was the 59th annual carnival. I have been dancing through the streets for the past few years alongside our Beavers, Cubs and Scouts in the parade.

It's great to let go, remember your inner child, and stop worrying about what others may think of you.

What I love about the local carnival, procession, and the fair afterwards is the simple joy it brings. I also enjoy having fun and bringing out the inner child, dancing around the village in the parade and making others smile and laugh.

The carnival supports the local community throughout the year, which adds to the day's power. Local businesses and community groups, such as the Scout group, can showcase who they are and what they do.

I come away inspired by the power of a community coming together
