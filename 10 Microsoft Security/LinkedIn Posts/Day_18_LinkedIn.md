## LinkedIn Post (Stuart's version — published)

Sunday reflection

The week, as with most, has been a roller coaster; there have been some lows when you need to take a breath, be kind to yourself and reflect. How can I improve? What can I do next time? What have I learnt?

Then there are the highs, when I received great feedback from some of my discussions, and I have to celebrate being myself. Always wanting to keep learning and how I can add value are critical in all I do.

As we are now halfway through the year, I do love this time; it's normally when I make the bigger decisions in life. Maybe more sunshine and more time outside make the personal difference in me?

What will the rest of this year hold is my thought today.

But what made a difference this week?

Posting daily on LinkedIn is more challenging than I expected, and it has driven my learning forward.

My career and life experiences add real value to my security role.

Allowing my inner child out brings fun and laughter to others.

What made a difference in your week?
