# Rivoli Group — Structure

The Rivoli group of property businesses is run by Stuart alongside [[Jeanie Mann]]. Overall group target: **£20,000/month** from property income.

## Companies and brands

| Entity | Role |
|---|---|
| **Rivoli Properties** | Holding company for the group |
| **Rivoli Developments** | BRRR and small-scale development — conversion-led, no ground-up builds |
| **Rivoli Stays Ltd** | Serviced accommodation (SA) in Burton upon Trent and Derby — see [[Rivoli Stays Overview]] |
| **Rivoli Property Management** | Landlord-facing Rent-to-Rent brand targeting tired/accidental landlords within 20 miles of DE11 — see [[Rivoli Property Management — Sister Brand]] |

## Rivoli Developments detail

- Core strategy: converting underused office/light-industrial buildings into 1- and 2-bed flats for first-time buyers, using permitted development rights (Class MA)
- Minimum 20% gross profit target on conversions; 25% aimed for where possible
- Deal philosophy: "deals are made, not found" — a deal missing the margin or in a thin market isn't an automatic no, it's a starting point for negotiation; the one hard knock-out is no clean permitted-development route
- Uses a 3-stage deal-analysis process: **DCAS** (Deal Criteria Assessment Sheet) → **IPAD** (Initial Project Appraisal Document) → **MAC** (Market Area Comparison Report)
- Stuart is doing the "Property CEO Mentorship" course; worked deal example is 8 Archdale Street, Syston LE7 1NA (10-flat conversion)

## Rivoli Property Management — restructure in progress (as of Sept 2026)

Being reformed as its own Ltd company under Rivoli Properties Ltd, rather than remaining a sub-brand of Rivoli Stays Ltd. It keeps the "Rivoli Property Management" trading name. Full detail in [[Rivoli Property Management — Sister Brand]].

- Serviced accommodation (SA) — via Rivoli Stays

Target: building 15–20 repeat investors and sourcing deals consistently.
