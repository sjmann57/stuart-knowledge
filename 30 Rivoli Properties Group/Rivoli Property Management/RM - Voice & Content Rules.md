# Voice & Content Rules

Applies everywhere RM speaks — ads, website, and social content alike.

- **Tone:** direct, factual, no exclamation-mark hype. UK English.
- **Standard sign-off/CTA:** "Get in touch for a free evaluation." — adapt to pair with the phone number for the call/message goal, e.g. *"Get in touch for a free evaluation — call or message +44 330 120 0140."*
- **Imagery:** never use a real Rivoli Stays property photo for RM content — RM is about other people's properties. Source real photos from Unsplash and verify by visiting the actual unsplash.com page and pulling its real CDN URL — never invent a photo ID.
- **Hashtags:** 4–8 relevant hashtags for organic social; drop or adapt for web copy and paid ad formats that don't use them.
- **Claim verification:** any factual, statistical, or legal claim (a law change, a market stat, a competitor claim) needs verifying against a live, credible, named source before use — in ads or on the website just as much as in social captions. Don't publish an unverified claim; flag it for review instead of softening it into something technically-true-but-vague.
- **Never blur the two options** (guaranteed lease vs. variable commission) — see [[RM - Option 1 (Rent-to-Rent)]] and [[RM - Option 2 (STR Conversion & Management)]].
- **Never publish** the actual commission rate or profit model — see [[RM - Commission & Profit Model (Private)]].
