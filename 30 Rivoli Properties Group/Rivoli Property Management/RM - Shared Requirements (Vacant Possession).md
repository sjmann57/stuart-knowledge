# Shared Requirement — Vacant Possession

Both [[RM - Option 1 (Rent-to-Rent)|Option 1]] and [[RM - Option 2 (STR Conversion & Management)|Option 2]] require the landlord to deliver a **vacant** property.

- Arranging that — ending an existing tenancy, etc. — is the **landlord's job**, not Rivoli's, under either option.
- This should be surfaced **early** with any lead, rather than late in a conversation, so it belongs in the [[RM - Lead Triage Process (Stage 1)|lead triage script]] and ideally early in ad/website copy too.
