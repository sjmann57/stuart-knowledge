# Target Landlord Profile

- **"Tired and accidental landlords"** within ~20 miles of DE11.
- Geography: Burton upon Trent and Derby, possible Nottingham extension.

## Certainty vs. upside (used in lead triage)
- A landlord who wants **guaranteed income with zero risk or effort** fits [[RM - Option 1 (Rent-to-Rent)|Option 1]].
- A landlord comfortable with **variable income for potentially more return**, and engaged enough to want visibility into performance, is worth having [[RM - Option 2 (STR Conversion & Management)|Option 2]] surfaced to them.
