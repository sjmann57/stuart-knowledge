# Brand and Positioning

## What Rivoli Management is

Rivoli Management is the trading name **"Rivoli Property Management"** (RPM), the landlord-facing brand within the wider Rivoli property group run by Stuart Mann and Jeanie Mann. It was originally operated as a sub-brand of Rivoli Stays Ltd (company no. 16494642) and is being reformed (September 2026) as its own Ltd company under the Rivoli Properties Ltd holding company — see [[02 - Corporate Structure and Two-Option Offering]].

RPM recruits and retains **landlords**, offering to take over the management of their property so the landlord doesn't have to let it themselves or deal with tenants directly. It targets "tired and accidental landlords" within roughly 20 miles of DE11 (Swadlincote/Burton upon Trent area), with a geographic focus on **Burton upon Trent and Derby**, with possible extension to **Nottingham**.

## How it differs from Rivoli Stays

| | Rivoli Stays | Rivoli Management |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Guests booking a stay (contractors, families, event visitors) | Landlords deciding whether to hand SA management to Rivoli |
| Funnel goal | Direct booking on rivolistays.co.uk | Enquiry / discovery call for management onboarding |
| Voice | Warm, hospitality-led, benefit-first | Warm but consultative and evidence-led — still zero em dashes, still not salesy, but the "benefit" is financial and operational, not experiential |
| Proof points | Property photography, guest experience | Yield data, case studies, compliance credentials, process transparency |
| Primary channels | Facebook, Instagram | Facebook + Instagram ("Rivoli Property Management"), LinkedIn ("Rivoli Property Management" company page) |

This is a genuinely different product, audience and funnel from Rivoli Stays, so it is run as **fully separate infrastructure** — its own Make.com scenario, its own dashboard artifact, its own scheduled tasks — with one deliberate shared exception, the Make.com data store (see [[05 - Technical Architecture]]).
