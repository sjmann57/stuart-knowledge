# Rivoli Property Management (RPM) — Knowledge Base

This vault is the single reference point for everything known about **Rivoli Management / Rivoli Property Management (RPM)** — the landlord-facing brand of the Rivoli property group — as of **15 September 2026**. It was compiled from the live technical specification (v2.0, 13 September 2026), the Rivoli Management Command Centre dashboard and its underlying Make.com data store, and Stuart Mann's own stated context.

It sits alongside, but is distinct from, **Rivoli Stays** (the guest-facing serviced-accommodation brand) — see [[01 - Brand and Positioning]] for how the two relate.

## Snapshot as of 15 September 2026

- **Status:** live and operating. Posting scenario works across Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. Weekly content-draft task is live. Dashboard is a published Claude Artifact. Daily publishing is still a manual recipe, not an unattended task.
- **Posts published under `brand: "RM"`:** 3 (`rm-001`, `rm-002`, `rm-003`), all posted 10 September 2026.
- **Posts approved and queued:** 6 more, dated 15–25 September 2026 (`rm-2026-09-15` through `rm-2026-09-25`).
- **Case Study Library:** empty — zero consented landlord testimonials on file. Unchanged since 4 September 2026; the single biggest open Phase 0 item.
- **Biggest structural change since the original spec:** RPM is being reformed as its own Ltd company under Rivoli Properties Ltd, and now offers landlords **two options** (guaranteed-rent lease, or SA-conversion management commission) rather than one. See [[02 - Corporate Structure and Two-Option Offering]].
