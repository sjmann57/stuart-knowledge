# Content Strategy

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Sample angles |
|---|---|---|
| **The Switch Case** | Educate landlords on why SA (via management) beats traditional letting right now | Renters' Rights Act impact on ASTs; periodic tenancy risk; SA void/yield comparison |
| **Hands-Off Proof** | Show that "fully managed" is real, not a sales line | A day in the life of the Rivoli ops team; changeover/cleaning turnaround; guest vetting process |
| **Numbers That Hold Up** | Build financial credibility | Occupancy rate ranges, revenue-per-property benchmarks — only ever real, sourced figures, never invented averages |
| **Compliance & Risk** | Position Rivoli as the safe pair of hands | Gas safety, fire safety, EPC, the forthcoming short-term let registration scheme, insurance |
| **Social Proof** | Convert trust into enquiries | Landlord testimonials (with consent), before/after property transformations, tenure-of-partnership stories — blocked until [[16 - Case Study Library]] has entries |
| **Local Market Insight** | SEO and authority | Derby/Burton SA demand drivers (same contractor/employer pipeline Rivoli Stays tracks — Rolls-Royce, Alstom, Toyota, Burton Towns Fund) reframed as "why this property, this postcode, performs" |

A weekly plan should draw one post from at least three different pillars — never two "Numbers" or two "Compliance" posts in the same week. Landlord content fatigues fast if it reads like a brochure.

## Tone by platform

- **LinkedIn** (company page): longer-form, first-person-plural, consultative. Primary channel for The Switch Case, Numbers That Hold Up, and Compliance & Risk — landlords and property investors are disproportionately a LinkedIn audience. Native multi-paragraph posts are appropriate here in a way they are not on Instagram.
- **Facebook** ("Rivoli Property Management"): similar depth to LinkedIn but slightly warmer, more local-community framing. Good home for Social Proof and Local Market Insight.
- **Instagram** ("Rivoli Property Management"): visual-first. Before/after transformations, short testimonial quote cards, day-in-the-life content. Captions shorter than LinkedIn/Facebook; image or carousel mandatory (same JPEG requirement as Rivoli Stays).

## Posting cadence

| Day | Platform(s) | Pillar rotation |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | LinkedIn + Facebook | The Switch Case or Compliance & Risk |
| Thursday | Facebook + Instagram | Social Proof or Local Market Insight |
| Friday (optional) | LinkedIn | Numbers That Hold Up or Hands-Off Proof |

Deliberately lower cadence than Rivoli Stays — landlord acquisition is a longer, more considered sales cycle than a booking decision, and three well-evidenced posts a week beats five thin ones. Note: per [[08 - Dashboard (Command Centre)]] the "platforms" field on a record is informational/intended-audience only — it does not actually restrict which channels a post goes out to (see [[14 - Operational History Log]] §14.4).

## How content actually gets made (live process)

A scheduled task, **"Rivoli Management weekly content draft,"** runs Mondays and:

1. Researches current, dated sources (regulatory changes, SA market data, competitor positioning).
2. Drafts the next Tuesday/Thursday/(optional Friday) posts, rotated across Option 1 and Option 2 ([[02 - Corporate Structure and Two-Option Offering]]) and across at least three content pillars.
3. Sources an image for each post from Unsplash ([[17 - Image Sourcing Policy]]).
4. Fact-checks every factual/legal/statistical claim against a live source and applies the auto-approve rule ([[07 - Approval Governance]]).
5. Writes each record to the shared Make.com data store tagged `brand: "RM"` ([[06 - Data Model]]).

See [[15 - Post Log (Live and Queued)]] for what this has actually produced so far.
