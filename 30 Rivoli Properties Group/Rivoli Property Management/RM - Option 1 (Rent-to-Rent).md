# Option 1 — Rent-to-Rent

- A **3–5 year lease** at a **guaranteed fixed rent**, paid every month regardless of voids, damage, or market dips.
- Rivoli manages the property directly, or hands it to another operator.
- This is the **existing, proven offering** — the current rivolipropertymanagement.co.uk site (as of Sept 2026) covers only this option, structured as a single page: Home / Benefits / About Us / How It Works / Contact.
- Fits landlords who want **certainty with zero risk or effort** (see [[RM - Target Landlord Profile]]).
