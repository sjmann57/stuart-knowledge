# Relationship to Rivoli Stays & Developments

Rivoli runs four related entities/brands — this note is the map between them.

| Entity | Focus | Relationship to RM |
|---|---|---|
| **Rivoli Properties** | Holding company | Parent of RM (and, going forward, of RM's own Ltd) |
| **Rivoli Stays Ltd** | Owned serviced-accommodation portfolio (Burton upon Trent, Derby) | RM's Option 2 runs *on* Rivoli Stays' operating systems (Guesty Lite, PriceLabs, CheKin, SuiteOp) but manages *other people's* properties, not Rivoli Stays' own. RM was previously a sub-brand of Rivoli Stays Ltd. |
| **Rivoli Developments** | BRRR and small-scale development (office/light-industrial → flats conversions) | Separate strategy — acquisition/development, not landlord management. No direct operational overlap with RM. |
| **Rivoli Property Management (RM)** | Landlord-facing Rent-to-Rent + STR management brand | This entity. |
