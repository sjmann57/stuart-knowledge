# Ad Campaign Plan

> Facts current as of Sept 2026 — check with Stuart if reviewing this much later; priorities and budget shift fast at this stage.

- **Channels:** Facebook + Instagram first; Google Ads planned later; LinkedIn still under evaluation.
- **Budget:** starts at £10/week, rising to £20/week — a lean, test-and-learn budget. Favour a small number of tightly targeted variants over a broad scattershot set.
- **Conversion goal:** a **call or message** — not a form fill or booking link at this stage. Every CTA should point to calling or messaging **+44 330 120 0140**.
- **Geography:** centred on Burton upon Trent and Derby, possibly extending to Nottingham.
- **Trading name:** stays "Rivoli Property Management" throughout — no rebrand.

## Sequencing
Organic-first: general unpaid social posts pushed out before any paid ad spend. Paid ads (Stage 2b) come once organic messaging is established.
