# Brand & Trading Name

- Trades as **"Rivoli Property Management"** — no rebrand alongside the Ltd company reform.
- Landlord-facing brand targeting **tired and accidental landlords** within roughly 20 miles of DE11 (Swadlincote).
- Geographic focus: **Burton upon Trent and Derby**, with possible extension to **Nottingham**.
- Domain: **rivolipropertymanagement.co.uk** — separate from rivolistays.co.uk, already owned.
- **Hard rule:** RM content is always about *other people's* properties, never Rivoli Stays' own portfolio, photos, or numbers.
