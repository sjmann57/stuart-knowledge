# Guest Demand Avatar

Used to judge whether a landlord's property/location fits [[RM - Option 2 (STR Conversion & Management)|Option 2]].

## Primary avatar
Contractors and business travellers near major regional employers — the kind of corridor that includes:
- Rolls-Royce Raynesway
- Alstom Litchurch Lane
- Toyota Burnaston-type sites

This is the same avatar Rivoli Stays already sees for its owned SA portfolio.

## Secondary avatar
Leisure/tourism — worth widening to where the specific location supports it (an attraction, event venue, transport hub). This is a **judgement call each time**, not a lookup table.
