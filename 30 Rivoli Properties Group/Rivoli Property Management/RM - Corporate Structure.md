# Corporate Structure

Rivoli Property Management (RM) sits under **Rivoli Properties Ltd** (the group holding company).

- Previously operated as a sub-brand of **Rivoli Stays Ltd**.
- Being reformed (from Sept 2026) as its **own Ltd company** under Rivoli Properties Ltd, rather than staying under Rivoli Stays.
- The Ltd company formation itself happens **when the first management deal is signed** under the new structure — not before. Until then, RM keeps operating and marketing under the existing setup.
- Keeps the **"Rivoli Property Management"** trading name throughout — no rebrand planned.

## Status log
- **Sept 2026** — reform initiated; site and campaign work under way; Ltd formation still pending first signed deal.
