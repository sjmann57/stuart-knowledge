# Lead Triage Process (Stage 1)

> Currently a **manual process Stuart runs himself**. This is Claude's current best understanding of how to sort a lead, not settled doctrine — expect it to evolve as real conversations teach more. Every recommendation is a draft for Stuart's judgement, not an autonomous decision.

## Inputs
Whatever's known about the lead — property address/type/area, what the landlord has said about their situation, what they seem to want (certainty vs. upside), any other context.

## Working logic
1. **Demand check for the property's location.** Does it fit the [[RM - Guest Demand Avatar|guest-demand avatar]]? If yes, Option 2 is worth putting on the table alongside Option 1. If not, lean toward Option 1 — but don't rule out the broader leisure/tourism avatar where the location supports it.
2. **Certainty vs. upside.** See [[RM - Target Landlord Profile]] for how this maps to Option 1 vs. Option 2.
3. **Vacant possession.** Confirm the landlord can and will get the property vacant, and that doing so is on them — see [[RM - Shared Requirements (Vacant Possession)]].

## Output
- A short recommendation (Option 1, Option 2, or both, with reasoning)
- A drafted reply/message Stuart can send as-is or edit, in the [[RM - Voice & Content Rules|RM voice]]
- Explicit flags where confidence is low — ask Stuart to sanity-check before anything goes to the landlord
