# Rivoli Stays — Marketing and Guest Demand

## Guest-demand avatar

Rivoli Stays' existing guest-demand avatar: **contractors and business travellers near major regional employers**. This is used by [[Rivoli Property Management — Sister Brand|Rivoli Property Management]] as the baseline for judging whether a landlord's property/location fits its Option 2 short-term-stay-conversion offer, and can widen to a leisure/tourism avatar depending on the specific location.

## Content distribution

- Content distribution to **Reddit** was automated via Make.com, with Claude-adapted copy and backlinks to rivolistays.co.uk.

## Social publishing automation

Two Make.com scenarios handle Rivoli Stays' own social publishing (distinct from Rivoli Property Management's separate scenario — see [[Rivoli Property Management — Sister Brand]]):

- **"Rivoli Stays — Publish to Social Media"** (scenario ID 5857917) — always active, posts Rivoli Stays content daily
- **"Rivoli Stays - Integration LinkedIn"** (scenario ID 5969045) — LinkedIn integration

Rivoli's Make.com plan caps active scenarios at 2, and these two normally hold both slots. When the separate Rivoli Property Management publishing scenario needs to run, 5969045 is temporarily deactivated to free a slot, then reactivated afterwards — full mechanics in [[Rivoli Property Management — Sister Brand]].

## Important distinction

Rivoli Property Management's social content is about *other people's properties* (landlord acquisition) and always sources images from Unsplash rather than Rivoli Stays' own property photos — Rivoli Stays' own content and RPM's content are kept deliberately separate.
