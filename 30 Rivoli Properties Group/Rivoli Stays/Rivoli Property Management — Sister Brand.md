# Rivoli Property Management — Sister Brand

Rivoli Property Management (RPM) is included here because its **Option 2** offer is built directly on Rivoli Stays' systems and guest-demand model. Full operational detail on RPM belongs in its own knowledge base area — this note covers the relationship to Rivoli Stays specifically.

## What it is

A landlord-facing Rent-to-Rent brand targeting tired and accidental landlords within 20 miles of DE11 (Burton upon Trent and Derby, with possible extension to Nottingham).

## Restructure (Sept 2026)

Being reformed as its own Ltd company under Rivoli Properties Ltd, rather than remaining a sub-brand of Rivoli Stays Ltd. The Ltd company formation happens when the first management deal is signed under the new structure. It keeps the "Rivoli Property Management" trading name.

It gets its own site/domain — **rivolipropertymanagement.co.uk** — separate from rivolistays.co.uk, though built on the same platform (self-hosted WordPress, Divi theme/builder, GoDaddy hosting).

## The two-option offer

- **Option 1** — the existing Rent-to-Rent model: 3–5 year lease, guaranteed rent, managed by Rivoli or another operator
- **Option 2** — helping landlords convert an already-rented property to **short-term stays**, using **Rivoli Stays' systems/processes**, with Rivoli taking a management commission rather than a guaranteed-rent lease

Both options require the landlord to deliver a vacant property — arranging that (e.g. ending a tenancy) is the landlord's job, not Rivoli's.

## Link to Rivoli Stays

Option 2 targeting uses **Rivoli Stays' existing guest-demand avatar** (contractors/business travellers near major regional employers, see [[Rivoli Stays Marketing and Guest Demand]]) as the baseline for judging whether a landlord's property/location fits, and can widen to a leisure/tourism avatar depending on location.

## Social content boundary

RPM's social media content is about *other people's properties* (landlord acquisition), not Rivoli Stays' own portfolio — RPM content always sources images from Unsplash, never Rivoli Stays' own property photos, and posts only once every factual/legal/statistical claim is fact-checked to 100% confidence (unverifiable claims wait for human review rather than auto-publishing).

## Make.com publishing mechanics

RPM's publishing scenario (**"Rivoli Management — Publish to Social Media"**, ID 7257618) posts to Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn on every run. Because Rivoli's Make.com plan caps active scenarios at 2, and those slots are normally held by Rivoli Stays' own two scenarios (see [[Rivoli Stays Marketing and Guest Demand]]), running the RPM scenario requires temporarily deactivating Rivoli Stays' LinkedIn scenario (5969045), running RPM's scenario, then deactivating it again and reactivating 5969045.
