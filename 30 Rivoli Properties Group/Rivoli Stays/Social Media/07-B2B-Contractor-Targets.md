# Rivoli Stays — B2B / Contractor Targets

The B2B/contractor content pillar (one post per week, almost always featuring **Rivoli Nightingale** because of its Derby city-centre location) targets project teams and contractors working on major regional developments. This article is a running reference of the employers and projects used as content hooks — refresh with a web search each week since these are live news items.

## Primary Target Employers / Contractors

- **Rolls-Royce** (Raynesway, Derby) — submarine manufacturing facility expansion; **Balfour Beatty** is the construction partner. Over 1,170 skilled roles being created across the site over roughly eight years. Rolls-Royce SMR's "Pioneer Works" (£12m manufacturing/training centre) is separate and due to open Q4 2026.
- **Alstom** (Litchurch Lane Works, Derby) — Elizabeth line Aventra train production; first of 10 new trains rolled off in June 2026, nine more through 2026-2027. Almost 40 UK companies in the supply chain.
- **Toyota Burnaston** (Derby) — recruiting production/assembly operatives ahead of the GR Corolla launch.
- **Barratt Developments** — new £45 million factory at Infinity Park, Derby, ~200 jobs, sustainable housebuilding base.
- **Balfour Beatty** — Rolls-Royce's construction delivery partner at Raynesway; also runs its own early-careers programme (apprentices/graduates/trainees) that has been used as a fresh content hook when live.

## Secondary / Burton-side B2B Angle (Rivoli Mews)

- **Burton Towns Fund** — £23.8m regeneration programme: High Street regeneration, Old Brewery Quarter (Bass House, Carling House, the historic Water Tower into a heritage destination), a new leisure/entertainment complex, a food hall, and a Washlands Visitor Centre.
- **A50/A500 Growth Corridor** — being positioned as a nationally significant route, with potential to unlock thousands of jobs and homes across the region.

## Messaging Angle

The consistent B2B post structure:

1. Open with a fresh, specific piece of construction/employment news (a real headline, not invented).
2. Connect it to the general truth that project teams need somewhere better than a hotel room for multi-week stays.
3. Introduce Rivoli Nightingale (or Mews for Burton-side stories): 3 bedrooms, DE1/city-centre location, short walk from the station, full kitchen, fast Wi-Fi, private parking for two, self check-in.
4. Close with weekly/monthly rates available, no OTA fees, and the standard direct-booking CTA.

## How to Research Weekly

Before drafting a B2B post, run a fresh web search for the latest Derby/Burton construction and business news (Balfour Beatty, Rolls-Royce, Alstom, Toyota, Barratt, Kier, Morgan Sindall, Skanska, Tarmac, Galliford Try, BAM, VINCI, local councils). Only use what a search result actually states — never invent a statistic or headline.
