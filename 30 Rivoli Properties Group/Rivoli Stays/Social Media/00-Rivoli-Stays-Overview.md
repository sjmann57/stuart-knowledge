# Rivoli Stays — Business Overview

Rivoli Stays is a UK-based serviced accommodation business operating in the East Midlands, primarily **Burton upon Trent** and **Derby**. It offers stylish, modern, fully-furnished short-term and long-term lets positioned as a "home away from home."

## Business Model

Serviced accommodation with a strong strategic emphasis on driving **direct bookings** (away from OTAs like Booking.com and Airbnb) via its own booking platform at `booking.rivolistays.co.uk`. Every piece of marketing content should nudge the reader toward booking direct rather than through a third-party platform.

## Target Audiences (in order of emphasis)

1. **Contractors / business travellers** — primary audience; properties are pitched hard for work stays near major regional employers.
2. **Families** — secondary; sleeps 6, private gardens, proximity to family attractions (Alton Towers, Drayton Manor).
3. **Leisure / short-stay guests** — event-driven and weekend-away bookings.

## Corporate Structure

Rivoli Stays sits within a wider group of property businesses:

- **Rivoli Properties Ltd** — the holding company.
- **Rivoli Developments** — BRRR and small-scale development (office/light-industrial to residential conversions).
- **Rivoli Stays Ltd** — serviced accommodation (this knowledge base's focus).
- **Rivoli Property Management (RPM/RM)** — a newer, separate landlord-facing Rent-to-Rent and short-let-conversion brand. See [[16-Rivoli-Property-Management-RM-Brand]] — it has its own social content rules and is **not** the same audience or messaging as Rivoli Stays.

## Why This Matters for Social Media

Knowing the audience split and the direct-booking focus is the foundation for every piece of social copy: content should appeal to working professionals *and* families, and should consistently close with a concrete direct-booking incentive rather than a generic "book now."
