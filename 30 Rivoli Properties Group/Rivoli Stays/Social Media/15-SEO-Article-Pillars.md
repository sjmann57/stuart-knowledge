# Rivoli Stays — SEO Article Pillars

Four recurring content pillars used both for the WordPress blog rotation (see [[11-WordPress-Blog-Publishing]]) and for the weekly social-plan's "one SEO article suggestion" (see [[13-Weekly-Content-Plan-Workflow]]).

## 1. Contractor Accommodation Guide

Practical guides aimed at project managers/procurement staff booking multi-week stays for teams. Grounded in real, current construction/employment news (see [[07-B2B-Contractor-Targets]]).

Example: "Contractor Accommodation in Derby: A Practical Guide for Project Teams Working at Rolls-Royce, Alstom and Toyota Burnaston" — target keyphrase "contractor accommodation Derby".

Typical outline shape:
- Why the local project pipeline is growing right now (named, current developments)
- Why hotels stop making sense for multi-week stays (cost, no kitchen, no team space)
- What to look for in contractor accommodation (site proximity, parking, kitchen, Wi-Fi, weekly/monthly rates, self check-in)
- Cost comparison: serviced accommodation vs. a run of hotel rooms
- A named Rivoli property as the worked example
- How booking direct works and what it saves

## 2. Property Spotlight

A deep dive on one of the four properties, its setting, and who it suits best. Rotates through Nightingale, Mews, Leas, Brook.

## 3. Event-Based Accommodation Guide

Tied to a specific upcoming event (see [[08-Local-Events-Reference]]). **Publish 6-10 weeks before the event** — this is the key timing rule. An event only 3-4 weeks away is too close for this pillar; use it for social posts instead (see [[06-Social-Media-Content-Strategy]]) and save the blog-length treatment for something further out (e.g. an October half-term guide published in August).

## 4. Local Area Guide

Broader "what to do near [property]" content — restaurants, attractions, transport links, seasonal activities. Rotates Derby vs. Burton by week number (see [[11-WordPress-Blog-Publishing]] topic-rotation formula).

## How to Apply

When suggesting an article for the week, pick whichever pillar has the strongest, most current, most verifiable hook — don't force an event-based guide if no event sits in the right 6-10 week window; a contractor guide grounded in real fresh news is often the safer choice. Give: title, target keyphrase, word count, and a 5-6 bullet outline.
