# Rivoli Stays — Brand Voice and Messaging

## Taglines and Core Lines

- **Primary tagline:** "Discover Your Perfect Stay"
- **Welcome line:** "Welcome to Rivoli Stays"
- **Positioning line:** "Stylish, modern serviced accommodation designed to provide you with comfort and convenience"
- **Emotional hook:** "Experience the comfort of a home away from home"

## Tone of Voice

Warm, welcoming, professional, hospitality-led. **British English** (en_GB — "neighbourhoods", "personalised"). Aspirational but accessible: reaches for words like "luxurious", "elegantly furnished", "exclusive", "exceptional", "unparalleled" without becoming overly formal.

In practice on social media, the tone has evolved to be a bit more plain-spoken and direct than the website copy — warm and benefit-led, but shorter sentences, concrete local detail, and no corporate filler. See [[06-Social-Media-Content-Strategy]] for the applied social captions style.

## Hard Style Rule: No Em Dashes

**Never use em dashes (—) in any Rivoli Stays content.** Use commas, full stops, or restructure the sentence instead. This applies to all Rivoli Stays marketing copy, not just LinkedIn — it's a standing Stuart preference across every channel.

## Core Value-Proposition Pillars

Used as section headings on the website and as content pillars for social:

- **Prime Locations** — vibrant neighbourhoods, near attractions/business centres/transport links
- **Luxurious Comfort** — spacious, elegantly furnished, relaxing, homely
- **Fully Equipped Kitchens** — modern, all essentials, home-cooked meals
- **High-Speed Internet** — complimentary Wi-Fi
- **Customer Support** — dedicated team
- **Flexible Booking Options** — few nights to longer stays
- **Direct Booking Discount**
- **Parking** — at most locations

## Direct-Booking Messaging (central to the strategy)

- Best rates available, no hidden fees or commissions
- Exclusive discounts and offers not on third-party sites
- Mailing list subscribers get early access to promotions and personalised assistance
- Frames direct booking as "supporting our commitment to exceptional service"
- Standard closing CTA used across nearly all social posts: **"Book direct: rivolistays.co.uk | Link in bio"**

## Recurring Phrases and Language Patterns

- "Perfect for contractors, families, or business travellers"
- "Modern & stylish 3-bed home"
- "Self check-in"
- "Seamless and stress-free experience"
- "Exclusive community" (for the mailing list)
- "No OTA fees" / "no platform fees, no third-party mark-up"
- "A proper home for the team" (B2B/contractor angle)

## How to Apply

- Use British English spelling throughout.
- Match the warm-yet-elevated tone; avoid overly salesy or discount-feeling language — the brand sits in the premium-but-friendly space.
- Lead with one of the value pillars.
- Always close with a direct-booking CTA that names a concrete benefit (better rate, exclusive offer, mailing-list perk) rather than a generic "book now."
- Check every draft for em dashes before finalising.
