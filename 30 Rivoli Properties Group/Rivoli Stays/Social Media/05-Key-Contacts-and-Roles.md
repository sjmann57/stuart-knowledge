# Rivoli Stays — Key Contacts and Roles

## Jeanie Mann

- **Role:** Primary business contact and the public-facing name of Rivoli Stays.
- **Use for:** All outreach emails, LinkedIn messages, and customer-facing correspondence should be signed off as Jeanie Mann.
- **Why:** Jeanie is the primary contact guests and corporate clients interact with directly.

## Stuart Mann

- **Role:** Owner / Director, background role. Runs the Rivoli group of property businesses alongside Jeanie.
- **Use for:** Internal strategy, system setup, back-end/technical decisions (Make.com automation, dashboards, AI tooling).

## How to Apply

Any email templates, LinkedIn messages, or customer-facing outreach copy should be written in Jeanie's name and signed "Jeanie Mann, Rivoli Stays." Do not default to Stuart for customer-facing content — he is the operator behind the automation, not the guest-facing voice.
