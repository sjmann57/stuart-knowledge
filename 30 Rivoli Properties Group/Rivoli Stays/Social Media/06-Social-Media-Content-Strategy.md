# Rivoli Stays — Social Media Content Strategy

## Objective

Drive **direct bookings** through organic social media (Facebook + Instagram, plus a separate LinkedIn company-page stream). Every post should read as helpful/local first, promotional second (roughly 80/20 value-to-promotion).

## Weekly Cadence

3 to 4 posts per week:

- **Tuesday, 10:00** — always runs
- **Wednesday, 10:00** — always runs
- **Thursday, 9:00** — always runs
- **Friday, 10:00** — optional, only included when there's a strong event reason (e.g. an event happening that weekend or the next day)

## Weekly Content Mix (one of each per week)

1. **Property Showcase** — feature one property's amenities/setting.
2. **B2B / Contractor** — target contractors and project teams at major regional employers. See [[07-B2B-Contractor-Targets]].
3. **Local / Events** — tie a property to a nearby event for FOMO. See [[08-Local-Events-Reference]].
4. **Social Proof / Direct Booking Push** — returning-guest angle or a direct hard sell on booking direct vs. OTAs.

## Content Techniques

- FOMO around events ("dates are already moving", "book before the good dates go")
- Seasonal hooks (quieter September, school holidays, bank holidays)
- Question posts and local facts
- Storytelling over hard-selling
- 80/20 value-to-promotion ratio

## Caption Format (standard template)

- Hook line (question, local fact, or news item)
- 2-4 short paragraphs: context/event or contractor news, then the property's relevant features, then a light urgency nudge
- Always ends: **"Book direct: rivolistays.co.uk | Link in bio"**
- Followed by **#RivoliStays** plus 4-6 targeted hashtags (property name, location, event name, content-type hashtags)
- **Under 300 words**
- **Zero em dashes** — see [[02-Brand-Voice-and-Messaging]]

## Optimal Posting Times (Facebook/Instagram)

| Day | Best times (UK) |
|---|---|
| Monday | Avoid |
| Tuesday | 10:00 and 17:30 |
| Wednesday | 10:00 and 17:30 |
| Thursday | 09:00 and 17:00 |
| Friday | 10:00 (good, not best) |

## Property Rotation Pattern (observed from real history)

- Nightingale is used for both B2B/contractor posts *and* Derby-based local/events posts in the same week when the event is Derby-specific — this has precedent and is not considered repetitive.
- Mews is most often the local/events property for Burton town-centre events (most central) but also gets standalone property-showcase slots.
- Leas and Brook (both Branston) alternate between property showcase, family-angle, and social-proof/direct-booking content, and are the natural fit for Branston/Tatenhill-area events (e.g. Party On The Pitch).

## Images

Never invent an image URL. Always use a real `.jpg` fetched from a property page or already confirmed in the data store. See [[04-Website-and-Asset-Reference]] for the known-good URL list. Instagram rejects PNGs.
