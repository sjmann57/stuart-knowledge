# Rivoli Stays — Visual Identity

## Logo

Wordmark "Rivoli Stays" — the favicon/tile asset is named "Rivoli-Logo-Text-Gold", indicating a gold, text-based logomark. Current site logo file: `Rivoli-Stays-v2-3.png` (uploaded October 2025, suggesting a recent rebrand/refresh).

## Primary Brand Colour

**Gold** (per the logo filename "Logo-Text-Gold"). This is the signature brand colour and should be used as an accent in any marketing collateral, dashboards, or artifacts built for Rivoli Stays.

The Rivoli Social Dashboard artifact uses this palette in practice: `--gold: #C9A84C`, `--gold-light: #F5EDD4`, `--gold-pale: #FDF6E3`, paired with dark neutrals (`--dark: #2C2C2C`, `--mid: #6B6B6B`) and warm off-whites (`--light: #F8F8F8`).

## Photographic Style

- Bright, naturally lit interior shots
- Neutral / warm-toned palettes (greys, creams, light woods)
- Modern, uncluttered furnishings — clean lines, contemporary
- Hero imagery is interior-led (living rooms, kitchens, bedrooms) rather than exteriors
- Each property has a "Main Header" image used as its key brand visual on listing pages

## Layout / Design Vibe

Minimalist, gallery-style image grids; lots of white space; straightforward navigation; the photography does the heavy lifting rather than heavy graphic design.

## Social Proof Assets

Screenshot-style images of guest reviews (named e.g. "Rivoli-Brook-5-star-Review-Theresa.jpg") are already curated in image format, ready for reuse on social.

## How to Apply

- Use gold as the primary accent colour against neutral/cream/warm backgrounds in any dashboard, report, or collateral.
- Lead with bright, naturally-lit interior photography of the actual properties — the website's image library (see [[04-Website-and-Asset-Reference]]) is the canonical asset source.
- Keep layouts clean and minimalist; let the property photos breathe.
- **Instagram requires JPEG images** — never a PNG — when sourcing or generating an image URL for a social post.
