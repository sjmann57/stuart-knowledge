# Rivoli Stays — Local Events Reference

Local/events content is one of the four weekly content pillars (see [[06-Social-Media-Content-Strategy]]). This article is a living reference of recurring and known events near Derby and Burton upon Trent — **always re-verify with a fresh web search before using**, since dates and line-ups change year to year and this list decays.

## Derby-area Events (use with Rivoli Nightingale)

- **Derby Folk Festival** — early October, Cathedral Quarter (Old Bell Hotel, Chapel Street Arts Centre). 20th edition ran 9-11 October 2026.
- **Darley Park Weekender** — late August, Darley Park. Multi-day live music (2026 line-up included Billy Ocean, Marti Pellow, JLS, Sinfonia Viva).
- **Derby Caribbean Carnival** — mid-July, Osmaston Park.
- **Japan Fest Derby** — mid-July, Derby QUAD, Cathedral Quarter.
- **Derby Market Hall Summer Programme** — rolling live music nights, vintage fairs, family workshops through summer.
- **Derby Pride** — mid-September, Markeaton Park.
- **Elvaston Castle Music Festival** — late August, Elvaston Castle Country Park.
- **Derby County FC home matches** — Pride Park, August to May.

## Burton upon Trent-area Events (use with Rivoli Mews, Leas, or Brook depending on proximity)

- **Burton Regatta** — mid-July, Burton Regatta Fields; long-running historic event (160th edition in 2026).
- **Sonic Boom** — mid-September, Market Place, Burton; free town-centre live music festival.
- **Burton Caribbean Carnival** — early August, town centre.
- **English Open Solo Championships** — mid-August, Burton Town Hall; national brass/banding competition.
- **A Night In Kilkenny / The Kilkennys** — early September, Burton Town Hall; Irish folk/ballads.
- **Party On The Pitch** — mid-September, Battlestead Croft, Tatenhill — closest to Rivoli Leas.
- **Burton Albion FC home matches** — Pirelli Stadium, August to May.

## Seasonal / Year-round Hooks

- **School summer holidays** (roughly late July to early September) — peak family booking season; lead with space, garden, kitchen, and proximity to Alton Towers (~30 min) and Drayton Manor (~25 min from Burton).
- **August Bank Holiday** — last long weekend of summer; strong all-properties direct-booking push.
- **Christmas Markets** — Derby and Burton town centres, December; push festive break content from early November.

## How to Apply

1. Web-search for events in the next 2-4 weeks before drafting the local/events post.
2. Never invent an event, date, or statistic — only use what a search result states.
3. Match the property to the event by proximity (Nightingale → Derby events; Mews → Burton town centre; Leas/Brook → Branston/Tatenhill-area events).
4. Avoid repeating the exact same event angle two weeks running for the same post — if an event was already covered as an early announcement, a second post close to the event date can work as a "last chance" reminder (different angle, not a duplicate).
