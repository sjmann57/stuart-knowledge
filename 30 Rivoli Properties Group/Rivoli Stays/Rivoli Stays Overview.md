# Rivoli Stays — Overview

Rivoli Stays Ltd is the **serviced accommodation (SA)** business within the [[Rivoli Group Structure|Rivoli group of property businesses]], run by Stuart alongside [[Jeanie Mann]].

## What it does

Operates short-term/serviced accommodation lets in **Burton upon Trent and Derby**.

## Position in the group

Rivoli Stays is one of three operating strategies under Rivoli Properties Ltd (the holding company), alongside:

- **Rivoli Developments** — BRRR and small-scale conversion-led development
- **Rivoli Property Management** — a landlord-facing Rent-to-Rent brand, currently being spun out as its own company (see [[Rivoli Property Management — Sister Brand]])

SA is also one of the group's three investment strategies (SA, BRRR, commercial development), sitting alongside a target of building 15–20 repeat investors and sourcing deals consistently.

The wider Rivoli group has a stated income target of **£20,000/month** from property. This is a group-level figure, not an SA-specific one.

## Guest profile

Rivoli Stays' existing guest-demand avatar is **contractors and business travellers near major regional employers**. This avatar is used as the baseline when Rivoli Property Management assesses whether a landlord's property/location suits Option 2 (short-term-stay conversion) — see [[Rivoli Stays Marketing and Guest Demand]].
